# Pomobar privacy policy

_Last updated: 10 November 2026_

Pomobar is a timer that runs entirely on your PC.

- **No data collection.** Pomobar does not collect, store, share or sell any
  personal information.
- **No network access.** Pomobar never connects to the internet. There are no
  accounts, analytics, telemetry, advertising or crash reporting.
- **Local settings only.** Your settings (session lengths, alert style, window
  position) and a count of today's completed focus sessions are saved in a small
  text file on your PC, at `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Pomobar\settings.ini`. Uninstalling
  Pomobar from the Microsoft Store removes it.

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be published here before
the change takes effect.

Questions? Open an issue at https://github.com/GlenDubb/pomobar/issues.
